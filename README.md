# My-Tableau-project

A collection of Tableau data analysis projects completed as part of the Data Technician programme. This repository explores global health trends using the Gapminder Health dataset and music trends using Spotify audio features. 📈🎵

## 🗂️ Projects Included

### 🌍 Global Health Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard analysing health indicators across countries and continents.

The analysis focuses on:

- Life expectancy by continent
- Life expectancy trends over time
- Population distribution by gender
- The relationship between life expectancy and BMI
- Additional health metrics and comparisons

### 🎧 Spotify Features Analysis

An exploratory analysis of Spotify tracks and genres using audio features such as:

- Popularity
- Danceability
- Energy
- Valence
- Acousticness
- Instrumentalness
- Tempo
- Loudness
- Speechiness

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Tableau Public
- Microsoft Excel
- CSV datasets
- Data visualisation
- Exploratory data analysis

## 📁 Datasets

### Gapminder Health Dataset

The health dataset includes information about:

- Country and continent
- Year
- Life expectancy
- Gender
- BMI
- Blood pressure
- Cholesterol
- Cancer incidence
- Population
- Population growth

## 📊 Tableau Worksheets

The Global Health Dashboard includes the following worksheets:

1. **Life Expectancy by Continent**
   - Compares average life expectancy for each continent.
   - Uses the most recent year available.

2. **Life Expectancy Trend**
   - Shows life expectancy trends over time.
   - Highlights the five countries with the highest average life expectancy.

3. **Population by Gender**
   - Displays population distribution by gender.
   - Includes an interactive country filter.

4. **Life Expectancy vs BMI**
   - Uses a scatter plot to explore the relationship between BMI and life expectancy.
   - Countries are represented as individual points and coloured by continent.

5. **Additional Visualisation**
   - Explores further health or population trends from the dataset.

## 🔎 Key Findings

- 🌍 Life expectancy varies considerably between continents.
- 📉 Africa has the lowest average life expectancy compared with the other continents in the analysis.
- 📈 Life expectancy trends can highlight differences in health outcomes between countries.
- ⚖️ The scatter plot helps explore possible relationships between BMI and life expectancy.
- 👥 Population distribution by gender varies between countries.
- 🎵 Spotify genres show differences in popularity, energy, danceability and valence.
- 🔊 Audio features can help identify patterns in the characteristics of popular music.

## 🏥 Potential Use in Healthcare

The health dashboard could support organisations such as the NHS and global health agencies by helping them:

- Identify regions with lower life expectancy.
- Compare health outcomes across countries.
- Investigate potential causes of health inequalities.
- Prioritise resources and future interventions.
- Monitor changes in population and health indicators over time.
- Support evidence-based planning and decision-making.
